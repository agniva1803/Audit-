# Unit Economics

## What a converted lead is worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits. Assume:
- Average deal size: $3,000 (one-time credit purchase, based on $500–$8,000 range)
- Gross margin on credits: ~25% (buying at 60–70% of list, selling at 80–85%)
- Gross profit per deal: ~$750
- Repeat purchase rate at 6 months: ~35%
- LTV (2-year): $750 + (0.35 × $750) = ~$1,012

**A converted lead is worth ~$1,000 LTV to Credex.**

## CAC by channel

| Channel | Cost | Estimated conversions/month | CAC |
|---|---|---|---|
| HN / organic content | $0 (time only) | 3–5 | ~$0 cash |
| Direct LinkedIn outreach | $0 | 2–4 | ~$0 cash |
| Newsletter sponsorship | $500–$2,000/drop | 5–15 | $100–$400 |
| Paid Twitter/X | $1,000/mo | 3–8 | $125–$333 |

At $0 CAC channels, every conversion is pure margin. At newsletter CAC of $200, LTV/CAC = 5x — healthy for B2B SaaS.

## Conversion funnel math

```
Visitors:              10,000 / month
Audit completed:        20%  → 2,000 audits
Email captured:         25%  → 500 leads
Consultation booked:     8%  → 40 calls
Credit purchased:        30% → 12 customers
Revenue (avg $3,000):        → $36,000 MRR after 12 months at steady state
```

The tool is profitable when:
- Email capture rate > 15% (we show value first, so this is achievable)
- Consultation booking rate > 5% (only for high-savings cases — qualified intent)
- Close rate > 20% on consultations (Credex can close with a concrete discount)

## What would have to be true for $1M ARR in 18 months

$1M ARR = ~$83k/month revenue = ~28 customers/month × $3,000 avg deal

**Math:**
- Month 1–3: 500 audits/month → 5 customers/month → $15k MRR
- Month 4–9: 2,000 audits/month (HN + newsletter traction) → 20 customers/month → $60k MRR
- Month 10–18: 5,000 audits/month (SEO + referral loop compounding) → 28 customers/month → $84k MRR

**What has to be true:**
1. The viral loop works — result pages get shared (unique URLs + good OG tags designed for this)
2. One newsletter pickup in first 60 days drives 2,000+ audits in a spike
3. Conversion from audit → email capture holds above 20% (value-first, no login helps)
4. Credex sales can handle 20+ consultations/month by month 6 (1 FTE)
5. Pricing data stays current — a single wrong number undermines trust and kills word-of-mouth

**Key risk:** The audit result is the product. If it's wrong, people share "SpendWise told me to cancel Cursor but I need it" → negative word-of-mouth. Pricing accuracy is the moat.

Not a home run at $1M ARR — but the tool is a distribution engine for the credit business, not a standalone SaaS. Its value is in deal flow, not its own margin.

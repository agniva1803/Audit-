## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**  
A: Yes. No credit card, no trial, no login required. You enter your stack, get your audit, done. We make money when teams with big savings use Credex credits — but we show you the honest result regardless.

**Q: How do you know the pricing data is accurate?**  
A: Every number in the audit engine traces to an official vendor pricing page with a verification date. We check weekly. The source file is public in our GitHub repo (`PRICING_DATA.md`).

**Q: What do you do with my email if I share it?**  
A: We send you a copy of the audit report. If your savings are over $500/month, a Credex advisor may reach out with a custom credit offer. We don't sell your email or send marketing spam.

**Q: What is Credex?**  
A: Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits — Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise — sourced from companies that overforecast. SpendWise AI is a free tool we built because most teams don't know they're overspending. We surface the problem; Credex is one way to solve it.

**Q: Why should I trust the recommendations?**  
A: The audit engine uses deterministic rules, not AI guesswork. Every recommendation includes a one-sentence reason with specific numbers. If you disagree with our reasoning, the source data is public. We'd rather be right than sell you a consultation you don't need.

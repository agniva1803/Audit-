## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes. No credit card, no login, no trial period. The audit is fully free. We're Credex — we make money when teams with large savings opportunities buy AI credits through us. If your stack is already optimal, we'll tell you that and won't push anything.

**Q: What data do you collect?**
A: You enter which AI tools you use, what plan, and how many seats. We don't ask for your name, company, or email until after you've seen your results — and even then it's optional. We don't sell your data.

**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**
A: Every price is verified against official vendor pricing pages weekly. Sources are cited in our public PRICING_DATA.md on GitHub. If a number is wrong, open an issue and we'll fix it within 24 hours.

**Q: Why should I trust your recommendations?**
A: Every recommendation shows its reasoning with the actual math. We also tell you when you're spending well — an auditor that only surfaces problems is trying to sell you something. We'd rather you trust us on the things that matter.

**Q: What's Credex?**
A: Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits — Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise — sourced from companies that overforecast or pivoted. This tool is free lead-gen for us: if your audit shows significant savings, we'll reach out with a credit offer. If not, you got a free audit and we got nothing. That's the deal.
